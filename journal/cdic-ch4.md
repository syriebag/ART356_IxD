# Critical Design in Context - Chapter 4: Criticism, Function, and Discipline

In summary, what I've learned from this chapter is the discourse on how critical design should be critiqued. The author, Matt Malpass, insists on creating new vocabularies and terminologies used to discuss critical design, rather than inherently using the same terms from critiquing art and design. 

Additionally, function played a huge role in identifying how critical design is different from other forms of design.
