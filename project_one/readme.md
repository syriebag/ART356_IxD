# Project One: FUNity
🪞 [Link to FUNity](https://syriebag.github.io/ART356_IxD/project_one/index.html)

This project was inspired by Professor Tyler's comment about his appreciation of students having profile pictures in Canvas. 
With this, I thought, what if there's an avatar random generator that people can screenshot and use as their profile picture?

## Moodboard
<img width="600" height="400" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-21 at 9 21 54 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/84bfe1e8-15ca-4d9d-880d-b60d06f8eb66" />

Creating a moodboard for this project was quite difficult, as I am not well-versed in game design. It was also challenging to pick an aesthetic that would best engage diverse demographics of users. But after looking into graphics, I came across this fun hand-drawn illustration of vanity items. This solidified the theme I went for, and I paired it with a light yellow background, #FFF4DC, to mimic a paper color. I used blue as an accent color, as it is complementary to yellow, and also to mimic the color of a mirror. I kept the graphics in black for the drawing effect. I also looked for a handwritten font type, **Just Breathe**, to go along well with the overall design.
